How It Works

Drive. Score. Earn.

No hardware. No seed phrases. No crypto knowledge needed. Just drive safely and let the protocol handle the rest.

01

Drive

The app auto-detects your driving using phone sensors. No manual start, no dongles. Just drive normally.

02

Build Your Score

Every trip builds your on-chain Safety Score (0–1000). Speed, braking, cornering, phone usage — all measured, all verified.

03

Own Your Identity

Your Driving Identity lives on-chain. Portable across insurers, employers, and platforms. You control who sees it.

04

Earn Revenue

Stake DVX and earn weekly distributions in WETH + USDC from real protocol revenue. Not emissions. Not inflation.

Why DrivX

Not another drive-to-earn token.

Most crypto driving projects print tokens as rewards. When emissions slow, the token dies. DrivX is structurally different.

Real Revenue, Not Emissions

Stakers earn WETH + USDC from trading fees, insurance verification fees, and marketplace fees. No new tokens are ever minted.

Fixed Supply, Deflationary

100B DVX. No mint function. Buyback-and-burn reduces supply permanently. Every burn is on-chain and irreversible.

No Team Tokens

Zero pre-allocation. 100% of supply into the liquidity pool. Team funded from 20% treasury. All wallets published at launch.

On-Chain Identity

Your Safety Score is portable, verifiable, and composable. No platform can revoke it. No company can lock it. You own it.

Score-Weighted Yield

Revenue share = stake × Safety Score. A safe driver with 1K DVX earns more than a speculator with 10K DVX and no score.

Immutable Economics

The 70/20/10 revenue split is hardcoded. No admin keys. No governance can change it. Verified on Basescan.
